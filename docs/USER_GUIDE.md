# User guide

Students register through **Create student account**, sign in, select **New complaint**, and provide a concise title, description, and location. The dashboard lists their reports; select one to see its current status and history.

Staff sign in with their staff account. Their work queue contains only complaints assigned to them. Open an item, choose the available next status, add a progress note, and save.

Administrators sign in to view the complete queue. Filter by text, status, or priority; open an item to assign it to an active staff member or progress it through an allowed status.

Use **Sign out** when finished. A closed complaint cannot be reopened through the portal.
